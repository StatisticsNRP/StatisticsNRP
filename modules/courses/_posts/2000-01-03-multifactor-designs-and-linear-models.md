## Design & Analysis of Experiments. Part 2: Multifactor Designs and Linear Models

-   **Presenter:** Statistical Services Centre
-   **Available to:** All NBI-based (QIB, TSL, EI, JIC) staff and students
-   **Priority for students:** Mandatory (MSc) Highly Recommended (PhD)
-   **Dates:** 16, 17, 18 June 2026 (Parts 1-3) 10.00-13.00

## Learning objectives

-   Design a trial with blocking to evaluate treatments with a factorial structure
-   Conduct an ANOVA for analysing a factorial treatment structure and report the results
-   Describe and explain the use of linear models in the analysis of experiments.
-   Conduct an ANOVA for analysing experiments with both categorical and continuous predictors and report the results
-   Explain the need for a split-plot design and conduct an appropriate ANOVA
-   Recognise the presence of pseudo-replication in experimental data and conduct an appropriate analysis
