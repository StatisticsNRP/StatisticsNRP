## Design & Analysis of Expriments. Part 3: Linear Mixed Models for Complex Designs

-   **Presenter:** Statistical Services Centre
-   **Available to:** All NBI-based (QIB, TSL, EI, JIC) staff and students
-   **Priority for students:** Highly Recommended (PhD)
-   **Dates:** 16, 17, 18 June 2026 (Parts 1-3) 10.00-13.00

## Learning objectives

-   Explain the difference between a fixed and random effect and illustrate the consequences of choosing one effect over the other
-   Distinguish between nested and crossed random effects
-   Fit linear mixed models with multiple random effects, either nested or crossed, in common experimental scenarios and interpret the results
-   Use a linear mixed model to analyse data from a cross-over design
-   Use residuals to check the assumptions underlying a linear mixed model and recognise the limitations of such a residual analysis
-   Analyse repeated measures using a random coefficients model
