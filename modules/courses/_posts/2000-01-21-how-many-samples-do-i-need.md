## How many samples do I need?

-   **Presenter:** George Savva (QIB)
-   **Available to:** All NBI-based (QIB, TSL, EI, JIC) staff and students
-   **Priority for students:** Highly Recommended
-   **Dates/Venue:** 30 June 2026.  Chris Lamb lecture theatre, JIC.

## Learning objectives

-   Revision of: hypothesis testing and estimation, p-values, type I and type II errors, confidence intervals.
-   Understand the principle behind sample size calculations for various common study designs
-   Know how to use R to estimate power, precision and sample size using simulation or supplied analytic tools, for a range of designs
-   Know how to identify, discover and interpret the inputs needed for a sample size calculation
-   Apply the principles learned for simple designs apply to more complex study designs
-   Reduce the sample size needed for a particular research objective
