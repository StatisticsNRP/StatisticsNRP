-   **Presenter:** UEA SCI PPD programme (2 days)
-   **Available to:** All UEA and NBI-based (QIB, TSL, EI, JIC) staff and students
-   **Priority for students:** Highly recommended for UEA students
-   **Dates/Venue:** Part 1: 13 Jan 2026 09:00 - 17:00 (TPSC 0.1); Part 2: 13/01/2026 09:00 - 17:00 (TEC 1.06)

## Overview / learning objectives

This 2 day course will familiarise you with Jamovi, an open source statistical analysis programme at the interface with R. Using Jamovi, 
we will introduce a number of statistical methods including:

-   Descriptive statistics: graphical and numerical summarisation of data
-   The normal distribution, estimation and confidence intervals
-   Hypothesis testing for numerical outcomes: single-sample and two-sample comparisons; t-tests; contingency tables and Chi-squared tests
-   ANOVA for one-way designs and for two-way designs
-   Non-parametric statistical tests
-   Simple linear regression
-   Multiple regression and non-linear regression
-   General linear models and an introduction to generalised linear models.
