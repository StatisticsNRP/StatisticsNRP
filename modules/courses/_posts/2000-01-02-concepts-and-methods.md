## Design & Analysis of Experiments. Part 1: Key Concepts & Methods

-   **Presenter:** Statistical Services Centre
-   **Available to:** All UEA students, including NBI-based students
-   **Priority for students:** Mandatory (MSc) Highly Recommended (PhD)
-   **Dates:** 2-6 Mar 2026, repeated on 18-22 May 2026

## Learning objectives

-   Import and manipulate data in the R software
-   Describe the role of the Normal distribution in statistical inference
-   Conduct statistical hypothesis tests for comparing means of 2 different treatment groups
-   Layout an experiment using common trial designs: completely randomised design (CRD) and randomised complete block design (RCBD)
-   Conduct ANOVA to compare means of treatment groups in common trial designs: CRD, RCBD and when the experimental layout is unbalanced
-   Analyse a linear trend
