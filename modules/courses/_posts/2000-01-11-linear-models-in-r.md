## SCI5RG2Y; Linear Models in R

-   **Presenter:** UEA SCI PPD programme (1 days)
-   **Available to:** All UEA students, including NBI-based students
-   **Priority for students:** Highly recommended for UEA students
-   **Dates/Venue:** 02 Feb 26; 9am - 4pm; Venue: UEA CAP 2.25

## Overview / learning objectives

Aimed at people who have a prior knowledge of univariate statistics (e.g. knowledge of t-tests, chi-squared tests, p-values and confidence intervals), this course aims to provide an understanding of how to create and interpret linear models in R.

- Demonstrate how to use the software R via Rstudio to carry out linear models
- How to perform and interpret model diagnostics, checking model fit, model selection and making predictions - and the packages available that can facilitate these processes.
- Fitting models with continuous, categorical variables and interaction terms
- Generalised linear models for non-normally distributed data
