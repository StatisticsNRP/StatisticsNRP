## A short introduction to the design and analysis of experiments

This is a three hour overview of the importance of experimental design, and key ideas that are common to many designs across fields of study.  We discuss common mistakes that can have catastrophic consequences for scientific validity, and how to avoid them.

The role of statistics in contemporary science is described, some basic concepts in statistical reporting, how we should approach statistical analysis, the resources available to you and how to engage with experimental design anda analysis throughout your period of study.

This course is important for all new students, and will contextualise the detailed work that follows in the statistics training programme.
