## Statistical Analysis and Experimental Design

-   **Presenter:** George Savva (Quadram Institute Bioscience)
-   **Available to:** All NBI PhD students and NRP-DTP students
-   **Priority for students:** Mandatory
-   **Dates:** 8 Jan 2026, repeated on 29 Jan 2026

A three hour overview of the importance of experimental design, and key ideas that are common to experimental designs across fields of study.

We discuss common mistakes that can have catastrophic consequences for scientific validity, and how to avoid them.

The role of statistics in contemporary science is described, some concepts in statistical reporting, how we should approach statistical analysis, the resources available to you and how to engage with experimental design and analysis throughout your period of study.

This course is important for all new students, and will contextualise the detailed work that follows in the rest of the statistics training programme.
