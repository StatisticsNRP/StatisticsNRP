Statistics is a big topic, individual needs will vary depending on field, and it’s completely normal not to know where your own gaps are, particularly early in your career.

This tool is a way for you to check in with yourself across different areas of statistical thinking and practice, 
notice what you already feel confident about, and spot the areas where you should prioritise further support or training.  It is not a technical quiz, but a way to think about how you approach statistics and respond to challenges when they arise.

Choose the statement in each row that best describes you.  At the bottom of each section, hit the button to see your score and suggestions for professional development.  If you don't know what the words mean, this probably means you should select the first option!

These scores are not recorded or submitted to anybody else, this tool is *only* for you to reflect on your own progress and professional development needs.

Reflect on your answers to identify specific needs, or use your results to decide which courses or other forms of learning will help you most.
