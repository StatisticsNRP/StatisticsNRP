This rubric will help you to assess your statistics training needs

|     Knowledge                                                                        |     4 – Proficient                                                                                                                 |     3 – Developing                                                                               |     2 – Basic Awareness                                                           |     1 – Needs Attention                                                     |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
|     Understands the role of   descriptive statistics                                 |     Uses appropriate summaries   (mean, median, SD, IQR) based on scale and distribution; explains choices                         |     Chooses descriptive stats   mostly correctly; some misalignment with data type or shape      |     Uses basic summaries   regardless of context                                  |     Overlooks or misuses   descriptives (e.g., mean of categorical data)    |
|     Understands how to derive   statistical hypotheses from scientific questions     |     Translates research questions   into testable statistical hypotheses (null/alt) with appropriate direction   and parameters    |     Attempts statistical   formulation but may miss key aspects (e.g., directionality, scale)    |     Can articulate scientific   question but not translate to statistical form    |     Cannot distinguish between   scientific and statistical hypotheses      |
|     Understands p-values and   confidence intervals                                  |     Consistently interprets both   correctly and explains limitations                                                              |     Generally understands but   occasionally misinterprets                                       |     Understands basic definitions,   but struggles with nuance                    |     Misinterprets or uses   incorrectly                                     |
|     Distinguishes between   association and causation                                |     Clearly differentiates and can   explain confounding, mediation                                                                |     Often gets it right but misses   subtleties                                                  |     Understands difference but   unsure when applying it                          |     Confuses or conflates them   routinely                                  |
|     Recognizes when statistical   assumptions are violated                           |     Routinely checks and adjusts   for assumptions                                                                                 |     Aware of assumptions and   checks most of the time                                           |     Has heard of assumptions but   rarely checks                                  |     Unaware of or ignores   assumptions                                     |
|     Understands effect size and   power                                              |     Reports and interprets effect   sizes and performs power analysis                                                              |     Uses effect sizes but doesn’t   always report or interpret power                             |     Knows about them but doesn’t   use                                            |     Relies only on p-values                                                 |
|     Identifies pseudoreplication                                                     |     Understands nesting, units of   analysis, and avoids duplication                                                               |     Aware of issue, sometimes   unclear on application                                           |     Knows the term but can’t   identify in practice                               |     Unaware of the concept                                                  |
