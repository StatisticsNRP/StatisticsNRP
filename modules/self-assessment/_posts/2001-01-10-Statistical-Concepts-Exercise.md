
# Statistical concepts self-assessment

This formative assessment presents a series of short research scenarios drawn from commonly encountered scientific and clinical contexts. 

The aim is to assess how you interpret statistical concepts and apply them in practice, rather than to test calculations or memorised definitions.

For each scenario, you will be asked to evaluate a set of statements or choose among possible analytical approaches. Some questions have a single best answer; others require you to select all responses that apply. After completing a set of questions, use the `Check answers` button to see feedback explaining the reasoning behind each option.

This activity is intended as a formative learning exercise. It is designed to help you identify areas that may need further study, and to build confidence in applying statistical reasoning as part of your research practice.
