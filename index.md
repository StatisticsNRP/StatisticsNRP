This site provides information and links to statistics training courses provided for staff and students at the Norwich Research Park.

It is particularly relevent for students on the NRP Doctoral Training Programme, but will inform other students and NRP staff interested in improving their statistics.  

We have also curated a list of externally available courses and other resources for self-directed learning.

## Why statistics?

Statistics broadly covers the design of experiments and the analysis, interpretation and presentation of quantiative data.

Statistics is central to designing meaningful experiments and drawing correct conclusions from them, as well as being able to critically read scientific literature.  So understanding statstics is important for all biological scientists.

## Which courses should I attend?

Your training needs will differ depending on your field of study and level of previous statistics education and experience.

We have outlined the contents of each course, some use cases for the material presented in each, and a short self-assessment for you to test your knowledge, skills and understanding.
