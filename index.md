This site provides information and links to statistics training courses provided for staff and students at the Norwich Research Park.

It is particularly relevent for students on the NRP Doctoral Training Programme, but will inform other students and NRP staff interested in improving their experimental designs and statistical analyses.  

We have also curated a list of externally available courses and other resources for self-directed learning.

## Why statistics?

Statistics broadly covers the design of quantitative studies and the analysis, interpretation and communication of the resulting data.  It is central to designing meaningful experiments and drawing the correct conclusions from them, as well as allowing you to critically read scientific literature.  

As a scientist, design of experiments and analysis, interpretation and communication of experimental data are the main tools of your profession, and so as a professional you should take responsibility for educating yourself in their use.

However, while statistical thinking is important for all biological scientists, statistics is a vast and sometimes complex topic, for which you will occasionally need specialist support.  Appropriate training will give you a good awareness of the limitations of your knowledge and when extra support should be sought.

## Which courses should I attend?

Your training needs will differ depending on your field of study and level of previous statistics education and experience.

On this site we have the contents of each course, some use cases for the material presented in each, and some self-assessment materials for you to test your knowledge, skills and understanding.
